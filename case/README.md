# Case - wooden shell v1

> **Note:** this first version targets the older LilyGO T-Display-S3 plan (portrait, eight buttons). It has not been adapted to the Waveshare AMOLED board yet (a one-hand grip with a thumbstick is being designed) and has not been run in Fusion. `case/dxf/` is generated by the script and is not committed.

Size **70 x 118 x 20 mm** (21.5 mm with the buttons), in a Game Boy-style portrait layout: the screen at the top,
D-pad on the left, A/B on the right, Start/Select below them, and the speaker grille at the bottom right.

## Generate it in Fusion
1. In Fusion, go to **Utilities > Add-Ins > Scripts and Add-Ins**. Click **+** next to "My Scripts" and choose
   the `case/fusion/PlayDeviceCase` folder.
2. Select `PlayDeviceCase` and click **Run**. It opens a new design and writes DXFs to `case/dxf/`.
3. To change a size, edit the constants at the top of `PlayDeviceCase.py`, then run it again.

## Parts
| Part | Material | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front plate | 3 mm birch ply or veneered ply | laser (`front_plate.dxf`) | Laser gives sharp D-pad hole corners. With a CNC, use 1 mm bit or square them by file |
| Frame | 14 mm hardwood (walnut, oak) | CNC (`frame.dxf`): profile + cavity through | Cut the USB-C slot (right wall) and power switch slot (left wall) by hand or 2nd setup. Their heights are in the script |
| Back plate | 3 mm ply | laser (`back_plate.dxf`) | 2.4 mm screw holes. Countersink if you use flat heads |
| Button caps | hardwood offcuts, 4.5 mm top + 1.5 mm flange | laser/CNC, glue top to flange | Or cut each cap from one 6 mm piece with a rebate. The D-pad has a 4 mm pivot nub underneath |
| Spacers | 5 x 5 x 6.5 mm blocks | saw | Glue them under the front plate. The perfboard screws to them |

## Stack (from the back, z in mm)
- 0-3: back plate
- 3-17: frame, with the battery (3-8) lying on the back plate
- 8.9-10.5: perfboard
- 10.5-15.5: tact switches
- 15.5-17: cap flanges
- 17-20: front plate
- The display board hangs from the front plate (7.1-17), with its glass pressed against the window.

## Check before cutting
- `SCREEN_DX` / `SCREEN_DY`: how far the screen is off the board centre. Measure your board. The default -2 mm is a guess.
- `USB_ZC`: the USB-C height. Hold the board against the front plate and measure from the glass to the connector centre (about 6.3 mm assumed).
- Clearance `CLR` = 0.3 mm per side. Use 0.2 if your laser kerf is wide. Do a test cut of the button holes first.
